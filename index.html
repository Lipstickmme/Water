Remediation & Desalination

Purification matched to what is actually in the water.

Reverse Osmosis (RO)

Pushes water through a semi-permeable membrane with ~0.0001 µm pores. Removes roughly 95–99% of dissolved solids, including lead, arsenic, cadmium, nitrate, fluoride and most PFAS.

Drawback: point-of-use units reject 1–4 litres of brine per litre produced, and strip beneficial minerals (remineralise for long-term use).

Both produce hypersaline brine that must be diffused to protect marine life.

Eco-purifiers & phytoremediation

  • Vetiver grass — deep roots take up heavy metals and nitrates from wetlands and runoff channels.
  • Water hyacinth — hyperaccumulates metals in constructed wetlands; invasive, so only in contained basins and harvest it.
  • Moringa seed coagulation — crushed seed proteins bind suspended particles, cutting turbidity and 90–99% of bacteria. Follow with disinfection.

Phytoremediation cleans source water over seasons. It does not make water drinkable on its own.

Rainwater & atmospheric harvesting

  • Divert the first 1–2 mm of each storm (first-flush) to drop roof debris.
  • Store in opaque, sealed, food-grade tanks with screened inlets.
  • Filter to ≤5 µm, then UV at ≥40 mJ/cm² (NSF/ANSI 55 Class A) or chlorinate.
  • Fog nets and atmospheric generators suit arid coasts; disinfect their output the same way.

Packaging Analysis

Why Water Water ships only in glass and paperboard.

MaterialProsConsVerdict
Glass Infinitely recyclable; chemically inert; no microplastic or BPA leaching; refillable 20–50 times; premium print surface. Heavy, so transport emissions are high on single trips; fragile. Used for local, refillable loops
Aseptic carton ~70–75% paperboard; light, so low transport emissions; blocks light and air; ships flat. Thin polyethylene and aluminium layers need specialist recycling facilities to separate. Used for long-haul & relief
Single-use plastic (PET) Light, cheap, shatterproof. Sheds micro- and nanoplastics into water; persists for centuries; true bottle-to-bottle recycling rates are low. Strictly excluded

The Water Water Free-Water Model

Water is free to drink. Advertisers pay for the bottle. Drinkers pay with a post.

1Sponsor buys print space on glass bottles and cartons
2Water is filled and distributed free
3Drinker scans the QR code on the empty container
4Shares a water-awareness post
5Account strength checked → voucher for a free 6-pack

Concept Explorer — ad-revenue formula

Assumptions: carton $0.14, glass $0.38 per unit incl. printing; refill wash $0.09; ~150 impressions per verification post. Adjust in js/app.js.

Concept Explorer — account strength & anti-fraud tiering

Compliance: incentivised posts must carry a clear disclosure (e.g. #ad / “I got free water for this post”) under FTC, ASA and similar rules, and must respect each social platform's terms. Collect only the minimum profile data needed for fraud scoring.
